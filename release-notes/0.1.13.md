What changed since 0.1.12:
- **Updates from inside the app** — the player looks for a new release on GitHub when it starts
  ("Check for updates" in Settings → About turns it off). The Windows setup and MSI installs and
  the AppImage update and restart in place; the Flatpak, the AUR package and macOS say a new
  version is out and link its release page. "Later" keeps the notice away for a day. Thanks to
  @TZANTOU. (#5, #7)
- **Keep the songs you jump over** — a new option in Settings → Playback → Queue and history. With
  it on, playing a song further down Up next plays it now and keeps the songs that were before it
  as the next ones, in their order. Off by default, which skips them as before.
- **Linux tray** — a left click on the tray icon shows and hides the window, and hovering shows
  its name. On KDE Plasma's Wayland session, hiding to the tray now takes the window off the
  taskbar, and the tray icon brings it back in front. The Flatpak can't do that part: there the
  window still minimises to the taskbar.
- **macOS** — the window's traffic lights sit over the sidebar, as in other Mac apps (thanks to
  @hanandewa5, #8), and there is a menu bar with Playback, View, Window and Help menus. A window
  minimised from the app's own button no longer freezes once it is restored, and the app keeps
  its own icon in the Dock while it runs.
- **Fewer sign-in refreshes** — an empty section in the Library or History was taken for an
  expired sign-in, so an account with one renewed its sign-in on every launch. (#4)
