Show your current YouTube Music song and artist on Discord, complete with album art and a moving Moosik equalizer. Friends can choose **Play song** to start the track in their own player.

To listen together, both people need this update. In **Settings → System → Integrations**, turn on **Discord Rich Presence** and **Host Listen along**, then play a YouTube Music track. A friend can click **Listen along** on your Discord profile to follow your pauses, skips, and seeks. They control their own volume and can leave anytime. Each person plays the music through their own Moosik app.

Song links and invites open through moosik.dev, with a download option for friends who need the app.
