# Moosik 0.1.16

Find that song you heard earlier, save a great queue, and rediscover your favorites.

## New

- **Listening history:** open it from the sidebar to search songs and artists, filter by date, and see when you listened. Every song has the regular playback and right-click actions. YouTube account history remains in its own tab.
- **Rediscover:** find forgotten favorites, five-star songs, and recent discoveries from your local listening history.
- **Smart playlists:** choose a Rediscover preset and click **Keep as smart playlist**. It appears in the sidebar and refreshes from your listening records.
- **Private playlists:** save streamed songs and local files together without publishing a YouTube playlist. Use **Save privately** in a song's menu, or save the current queue as a playlist. Duplicate saves are skipped.
- **Song transfers:** drag songs to the queue or editable sidebar playlists. Cmd/Ctrl-click and Shift-click select groups in ordinary song lists; selected queue songs can be copied together.
- **Stats comparisons:** listening-time changes and rising artists compared with the previous equal-length period.

## Notes

Listening history, private playlists, and smart-playlist rules stay on this Mac. History uses listens recorded by Moosik; this release does not import older listening activity or sync it across devices. Scrolling a long destination list while dragging and selection across paged grids remain future refinements.

## Update or install

Existing updater-enabled clients can use **Moosik → Check for Updates…**, then install and restart. For a first installation, download the Apple Silicon DMG and drag Moosik to Applications. The installer and update feed declare the minimum supported macOS version. Intel Mac, Windows, and Linux packages are not included.

Update archives are verified with Moosik's Ed25519 key. App signing now uses the pinned Moosik release certificate. This certificate is self-signed, and the app is not Apple-notarized: macOS may require first-launch approval, and Keychain permission can be requested again after updates. Keep credentials in Keychain; there is no need to grant every app access.
